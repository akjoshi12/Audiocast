# Audiocast promo: three storylines

**The one thing:** upload an ebook, get a full-cast audiobook, all on your own machine.
**Real facts we can use** (from EVAL.md): 97.6% word match over 6 chapters, 97.0% speaker attribution (P&P Ch. I), real-time factor 1.15 for a full chapter, nothing leaves the computer. Real screens: `assets/web-demo.mp4`, `assets/phone-demo.mp4`.

## A. "The Bennets" (recommended: a stranger follows a scene they know)
- **Device/form:** result first, then pull back. Cut/continuous pull-back.
- **Sees/feels:** Mr. and Mrs. Bennet argue over Netherfield in two clearly different voices, shown as colour-coded lines. The camera pulls back to reveal the web UI that made it, then the phone it syncs to.
- **First 2 s:** "My dear Mr. Bennet, have you heard that Netherfield Park is let at last?" starts playing, words lighting up in Mrs. Bennet's colour.
- **Beats:** hook (the scene plays) → tension (one flat narrator reading the same page) → reveal (Audiocast) → proof (upload, analyze, cast, render in the real UI) → payoff (phone, offline) → claim (97% of words spoken as written) → end card.
- **Look:** warm paper-and-ink ground in the book's world, speaker colours as the only accent. **Sound:** existing music bed, voices from the real render.

## B. "The cast list"
- **Device/form:** one object across apps, object-to-object chain.
- **Sees/feels:** a plain speaker tag, `Mrs. Bennet`, is the carrier. It leaves the paragraph, becomes a row in the cast panel, becomes a voice waveform, becomes a name on the phone's lock screen.
- **First 2 s:** a page of text with every quote mark flicking to a name.
- **Beats:** hook → tension (who is talking?) → reveal → proof (label, cast, render, sync, one each) → payoff → claim ("it never changes your book's words") → end card.
- **Look:** neutral light ground, the tag in one saturated colour. **Sound:** tick on each handoff, quiet bed.

## C. "Receipts" (the bold one)
- **Device/form:** a number lands, cut on the beat with split screen to one.
- **Sees/feels:** an honest audit. Each real measurement is earned by a scene: 97.6% of words spoken as written, 97.0% speaker accuracy, 1.15x real time, 54 GB, zero bytes uploaded. It also shows the worst line (a copyright marker read as babble) and says what is not fixed.
- **First 2 s:** a bare `97.6%` counting up over a waveform, cut hard on the first beat.
- **Beats:** hook (number) → tension (the worst line) → reveal → proof (three measured scenes) → payoff (full chapter plays) → claim ("runs entirely on your Mac") → end card.
- **Look:** monospace terminal-green on near-black is banned as a default, so: bright field colours that change every half beat, large type. **Sound:** beat-driven, cuts on the music.

## Assumptions (change any)
1920x1080, 30 fps, 35-45 s (the current cut is 75.6 s), no voiceover or captions, reuse `music.wav` (free) and the two real screen recordings. Nothing paid.
